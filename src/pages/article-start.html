Getting started · 3 min read

A calmer start. One enquiry at a time.

Changing the way your agency works can begin with the next enquiry, rather than a complete migration.

In this story

  1. Begin with a small, real workflow.
  2. Agree on the essentials.
  3. Bring information over at your pace.
  4. Review automations before relying on them.
  5. Use the demo to ask practical questions.

Begin with a small, real workflow.

Choose one common enquiry type and walk it through from the first message to a proposal. Use a trip your team understands well. A familiar example makes it easier to see what works, what information is missing and where a handover needs a little more care.

Agree on the essentials.

Decide which information every new enquiry needs: a contact, a destination, a date range, a group size and a clear owner. Add the details that matter for your agency. Start with a useful record and refine it as your team learns, rather than trying to design every possible case in advance.

Bring information over at your pace.

Paryatech’s current onboarding approach lets an agency start with new enquiries and bring existing customers, vendors and rate cards over gradually. Discuss your existing records in the demo so the team can explain the available approach for your situation.

Review automations before relying on them.

Walk through the follow-ups and reminders you want to use, and decide when a person should review the next step. Paryatech makes automation runs visible and reversible. A clear shared understanding of each workflow helps your team stay in control.

Use the demo to ask practical questions.

Bring your current tools, a representative enquiry and the questions your colleagues ask every day. Ask how assignments work, what the traveller sees and how you pause an automation. A useful demo should help you judge the fit for your agency’s daily work.

Start small, make the next step clear, then build from there.
