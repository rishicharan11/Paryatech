05 / Create

Make the proposal
feel like the trip.

Bring the itinerary and selected services into one proposal. Add ₹6,000 for the agency’s service to the ₹54,000 supplier cost: a ₹60,000 total, or ₹10,000 per person. Rohan sees one considered offer for the same six travellers and dates.

In the workspace Query → Proposals → Share in Communication
